Furby Simulator

Tap his head or ears to pet him, tickle his belly, or stroke his sides. On a phone you can shake it too.

Test a DLC

Fan project. Eyes and sounds load from the Furby Connect files in the Furby-ReConnect repo. Sound decoding is ported from a1800_codec. Furby is a trademark of Hasbro.

Turn your sound on. Furby's eyes and voice load from the internet, so the first few moves can take a second.
